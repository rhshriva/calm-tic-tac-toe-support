Calm Tic Tac Toe Offline

A quiet, private strategy game for iPhone and iPad.

Support

If you have a problem, suggestion, or accessibility concern, contact the developer by opening a support request on GitHub. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Open a support request

Privacy

Calm Tic Tac Toe Offline does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The game works entirely on your device and does not require an account or internet connection.

The computer opponent uses deterministic game logic running locally on your device. It does not use an online service or AI model.

If you contact the developer through GitHub, GitHub processes the information you submit under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

Compatibility

The app is designed for iPhone and iPad. For best results, keep iOS or iPadOS updated.
