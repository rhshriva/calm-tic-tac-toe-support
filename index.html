How can we help?

Report a problem, suggest an improvement, or share an accessibility concern by opening a support request. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not include passwords, account details, or other sensitive personal information.

Open a support request

Support requests are handled through GitHub Issues. A free GitHub account may be required to submit a request.

How to play

1. Choose difficulty

Select Easy, Challenging, or Unbeatable before starting a round.

2. Make your move

Tap an empty square to place your mark. The computer responds automatically.

3. Complete a line

Place three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win.

4. Play another round

Start a new round and continue tracking wins and draws during your session.

Difficulty levels

  • Easy: relaxed play with intentionally imperfect computer moves.
  • Challenging: a stronger opponent that rewards careful play.
  • Unbeatable: optimal local strategy; the best possible result is a draw.

Troubleshooting

  1. Confirm that you are tapping an empty square and that the current round has not ended.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  4. Install the latest available iOS or iPadOS update.
  5. If the problem continues, open a support request with the steps needed to reproduce it.

Accessibility

The game includes labeled controls intended to work with VoiceOver and supports responsive layouts on iPhone and iPad. If a control is unclear or difficult to use with an assistive technology, please open an accessibility support request so it can be investigated.

Privacy policy

Calm Tic Tac Toe Offline does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising or analytics SDK is included.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions are offered.
  • Gameplay runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection.
  • The computer opponent uses deterministic game logic on your device. It does not connect to or use an AI model.

If you choose to contact the developer through GitHub, GitHub processes the information you submit according to GitHub’s privacy statement. Only provide information necessary to resolve your request.

Policy effective date: September 11, 2026. This policy will be updated here if the app’s data practices change.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need internet access?

No. Gameplay is fully offline.

Does “Unbeatable” use generative AI or an online model?

No. It uses deterministic Tic Tac Toe strategy logic that runs locally on your device.

Does the app collect analytics or personal data?

No. The app does not collect analytics or personal data.

Are there ads, subscriptions, or purchases?

No. The game has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

The app is designed for compatible iPhone and iPad devices. Keeping iOS or iPadOS updated is recommended.

How do I report a bug?

Use the support button above and describe your device, operating system version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue.
